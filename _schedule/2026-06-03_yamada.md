topic: "Toward Verifiable AI-Generated AI Science: Lessons from The AI Scientist"
abstract: This talk surveys the emerging landscape of AI systems for scientific research, from coding agents and AI co-scientists to closed-loop discovery systems. I will use The AI Scientist, our recent Nature publication, as a case study for understanding how far current LLM-based systems can be pushed toward autonomous research when combined with tools, execution environments, search, and review mechanisms. I will discuss challenges in problem design, verification, hallucination, and transparency around these systems. The talk will conclude with open questions about how future AI Scientist systems should be evaluated, governed, and integrated into scientific practice.
